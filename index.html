What DocuForge does

A full document toolkit, not a single-purpose app

Most phone utilities do one thing. DocuForge ships three suites — PDF, images and files — and every one of them works with the network switched off.

15 PDF tools

Merge, split and compress. Protect with AES-256, organise pages, sign, add watermarks, edit text, or build a PDF from plain text or Markdown.

16 image tools

Hit an exact KB target, convert HEIC or WebP, crop to social formats, resize in batch, redact, remove backgrounds and strip EXIF and GPS data.

Scanner with auto-crop

Scan up to 12 pages in one go, straighten edges automatically, then apply colour, grey or black-and-white filters before saving as PDF.

Official photo sizes

17 document photo sizes across 15 countries — passport, visa and ID — at the right DPI, with an on-device face guide and a file-size ceiling.

QR, barcodes and archives

Scan or generate QR codes and eight barcode formats. Create ZIP archives and extract ZIP, TAR, TAR.GZ, BZ2, XZ and ZST safely.

Private by design

No analytics, no ad networks, no crash reporters, no social login. The app makes no network requests of its own — your files stay in local storage.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your phone

DocuForge has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs. Photos and PDFs are read and written locally; the app makes no network requests of its own. History keeps the last 30 results on your device, and one tap clears it.

FAQ

Common questions

Is DocuForge free?

Yes. The app is free to download and every tool is available without an account or a subscription. There are no ads and no watermarks on your output.

Does it upload my photos or documents?

No. All processing happens on your device. The app contains no analytics, ad or tracking SDKs and makes no network requests of its own.

Does it work offline?

Yes, with one small exception: the face guide used when making passport photos downloads its ML Kit model once through Google Play services. After that it runs on-device. Your images are never sent to Google.

Where are my files saved?

Results go to the app’s local storage, and to your gallery or Downloads only when you tap Save or Export. Deleting a result, clearing history or uninstalling the app removes them permanently — keep your own backups of anything important.

What is the History screen?

It keeps your last 30 results plus file metadata, locally. You can delete individual entries or clear the whole list at any time, and you can optionally protect it with a biometric lock.

Which languages are supported?

Ten: Arabic, English, French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish and Turkish. Arabic and Hindi interfaces run right-to-left and left-to-right correctly.

Does it add a watermark to my files?

No. Watermarking is a tool you can choose to use; nothing is added to your output automatically.

Stop emailing files to yourself

Install DocuForge and handle the PDF, the passport photo and the scan on the phone in your hand.
