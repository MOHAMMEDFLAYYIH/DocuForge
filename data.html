Your data

Everything stays on your phone

DocuForge has no account and no server. Nothing you process is uploaded, so there is no profile to close and no remote copy to request. Deleting your data means deleting it on your device — and that is entirely in your hands.

What is stored on your device

WhatWhereHow to remove it
Results you created (PDFs, images, archives) App private storage, plus your gallery or Downloads if you tapped Save or Export Delete the file in the app or in your file manager
History — the last 30 results and their file metadata App private storage, on device only History → delete an entry, or Clear all
Preferences — theme, language, biometric lock App private storage Change them in Settings, or uninstall the app
Originals you picked Wherever they already lived on your device Untouched by the app — delete them yourself if you want to

How to delete it

  1. Delete one result

    Open History, tap the item, choose Delete. The file and its entry are removed.

  2. Clear the whole history

    In History, tap Clear all. This removes the list and the app’s stored copies.

  3. Remove everything at once

    Uninstall DocuForge, or open Android Settings → Apps → DocuForge → Storage → Clear data. This deletes the app’s private storage, including history and preferences.

  4. Delete exported copies

    Files you saved to your gallery or Downloads live outside the app. Delete them from your gallery or file manager.

There is nothing to delete on our side. Because the app transmits nothing, we hold no copy of your files, no account record and no usage profile. If you uninstall the app, everything it stored is gone.

Before you delete: back up

Deletion is permanent. If a document matters, export it to your gallery, Downloads or another app first. DocuForge does not keep a cloud copy you can restore from.

Revoking permissions

You can revoke camera, photo or biometric access at any time in Android Settings → Apps → DocuForge → Permissions. The app will simply ask again the next time a tool needs that access, or stop offering the related feature.
