Frequently asked

A compressed file missed the target size. Why?

Compression works by reducing quality until the file fits, so some images—already compressed JPEGs, or very detailed photos—cannot reach a very small target without looking bad. The app stops before destroying the image. Try a slightly larger target, or convert to JPEG first with the Format converter.

An official photo size was rejected by a website

Most portals check three things: exact pixel dimensions, DPI metadata, and a maximum file size. In Official photo sizes, confirm the country and document type first — the app sets width, height and DPI together. If the portal still refuses it, email us the exact requirements and we will check the preset.

OCR is not recognising my language

Text recognition runs on-device. Open the OCR tool and make sure the right recognition language is selected; Latin, Arabic and Devanagari scripts are supported by the bundled models. Low light or angled photos reduce accuracy — rescan with the document scanner first.

Background removal left rough edges

The tool uses an on-device segmentation model plus an edge fallback. It works best on a clear subject against a plain background. Try the passport-blue or white output mode, and crop tighter before running it.

A password-protected PDF will not open

Use Protect / unlock and enter the existing password to remove protection. If the file uses a certificate rather than a password, the app cannot open it. We never receive your file or your password.

Where are my exported files saved?

Results are written to the app’s local storage and, when you tap Save or Export, to your gallery or Downloads. On Android 10 and later you can also share straight to another app without saving a copy.

Why is there no account or sign-in?

Because there is no server. DocuForge processes everything on your device, so an account would add risk without adding anything. Your files and history never leave the phone, and there is nothing for us to sync or recover.

How do I delete my history and files?

Open History and delete an entry, or tap Clear all. To remove everything, uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings. Full steps are on the your data page.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. The only network use is the one-time download of the ML Kit face-detection model through Google Play services, used by the passport-photo face guide. Everything else runs locally.

Still stuck?

Send us a message with as much detail as you can: device model, Android version, app version, the tool you used, and what you expected to happen. Screenshots help.

Do not send scanned identity documents, passwords or other sensitive files — we never need them to troubleshoot, and the app has no way to receive them anyway.
